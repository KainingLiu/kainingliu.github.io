MemoCat 记忆猫MemoCat

2026

常驻桌面的布偶猫语音智能体：在后台持续转写、区分说话人、判断场景，按用户定义的方式把课堂、组会和讨论整理成结构化报告，存入可检索、可溯源的本地记忆库；也可以随时开口和它对话。

A ragdoll-cat voice agent that lives on the desktop. It transcribes in the background, tells speakers apart, recognizes the scene, and turns lectures, meetings and discussions into structured reports in the format the user defines, stored in a searchable, source-traceable local memory. You can also just talk to it.

Uniforest 机器人Uniforest Robot

2026

自主搬运与搭建机器人。树莓派 5 上位机负责视觉识别、策略调度与底盘外环，STM32F427 下位机负责底盘、执行机构与 IMU，两者通过自定义协议通信。

An autonomous pick-carry-and-build robot. A Raspberry Pi 5 runs vision, strategy and the outer chassis loop; an STM32F427 board drives the chassis, mechanisms and IMU, talking to the Pi over a custom protocol.
