一句话介绍In one sentence

让 AI 模型像人一样拥有自己的记忆。（课题原名：用参数化向量库作为记忆媒介，探究具有原生记忆能力的 AI 模型。）

Giving AI models a memory of their own, the way people have one. (Formal title: using a parameterized vector store as the memory medium to explore AI models with native memory.)

要解决什么问题The problem

今天的 AI 聊天助手其实“记不住事”：它每次回答时，能参考的只有眼前这段对话的文字。聊得越久，要从头重读的内容越多，速度越慢、成本越高；超过一定长度，就只能把前面的内容忘掉。

Today's AI chat assistants cannot really remember. Each time they answer, all they can look at is the text of the current conversation. The longer you talk, the more they have to re-read from the start, so they get slower and more expensive, and past a certain length they simply have to forget the beginning.

市面上的“记忆功能”大多是在模型外面另配一个笔记本，由人写好规则决定记什么、什么时候去翻。模型本身并没有学会怎样记忆。

Most “memory features” on the market bolt an external notebook onto the model, with hand-written rules deciding what to write down and when to look things up. The model itself never learns how to remember.

我们的思路Our approach

  • 记住的是理解，而不是原文：模型把自己读懂之后的“印象”存进一块可以不断增长的记忆里，而不是一字不落地抄下来；
  • 记什么、何时回想，由模型自己学会：通过训练将原生记忆能力融入模型，不依赖外部规则；
  • 原文不在眼前也能想起来：即使对话内容已经被清空，模型依然能凭记忆回答相关问题；
  • 容易落地：对现有模型改动很小，可以沿用已有的训练和使用方式。
  • Remember the understanding, not the words: the model stores its own “impression” of what it has read in a memory that keeps growing, instead of copying text word for word.
  • The model learns what to remember and when to recall: training builds this native memory into the model itself, with no external rules.
  • Recall without the original text: even after the conversation has been cleared, the model can still answer questions about it from memory.
  • Easy to adopt: it needs very few changes to existing models and works with today's training and deployment pipelines.
