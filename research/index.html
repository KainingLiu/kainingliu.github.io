研究Research

我在中国科大数据科学实验室（LDS）从事人工智能记忆与持续学习的研究，导师为王文杰教授。

I work on memory and continual learning for AI at the Lab for Data Science (LDS), USTC, advised by Prof. Wenjie Wang.

研究兴趣Interests

记忆与持续学习Memory & continual learning
让模型原生地积累记忆、在交互中持续学习，而不是一次训练、永久冻结。Models that natively accumulate memory and keep learning through interaction, instead of being trained once and frozen.
自进化与主动学习Self-evolution & active learning
智能体如何主动理解、主动探索，并在使用中改进自身。How agents can actively understand and explore, and improve themselves as they are used.
稀疏计算与类脑智能Sparse computation & brain-inspired intelligence
借鉴人脑稀疏、高效的计算方式构建智能系统。Building intelligent systems that borrow the sparse, efficient computation of the brain.
世界模型World models
对真实世界、具身场景与多感官信息的连贯理解。Coherent understanding of the real world, embodied settings and multi-sensory information.

科研经历Research Experience

LatentTrace

2026.08 – 至今2026.08 – Present

课题负责人 · 中国科大数据科学实验室（LDS）· 导师：王文杰Project lead · Lab for Data Science (LDS), USTC · Advisor: Prof. Wenjie Wang

研究内容用参数化向量库作为记忆媒介，探究具有原生记忆能力的 AI 模型。今天的大模型只能把记忆放在上下文文本里，历史越长越慢越贵；本课题让模型把自己处理信息时的内部表示存入一块可持续增长的向量记忆，什么时候存、存什么、什么时候回忆都由模型在训练中学会，清空上下文后仍能凭记忆作答。

ResearchUsing a parameterized vector store as the memory medium to explore AI models with native memory. Today’s LLMs can only keep memory as text in their context, which gets slower and costlier as history grows. This project lets a model write its own internal representations into a growing vector memory, learning in training when to store, what to store and when to recall, so it can still answer after the context is cleared.

我的工作独立进行思路调研、模型架构设计、数据集构造和模型训练。

My roleIndependently carried out the literature survey, model architecture design, dataset construction and model training.

自进化智能体的理论研究Theory of Self-Evolving Agents

2026 暑期Summer 2026

访问学生 · 北京中关村学院 · 合作老师：陈祥Visiting student · Zhongguancun Academy, Beijing · Host: Xiang Chen

研究内容从理论上研究自进化智能体与冻结策略智能体在元学习与自适应能力上的差异：部署后参数固定的智能体只能依靠上下文适应新任务，而自进化智能体在使用中持续更新自身，两者在“学会如何学习”上的差别在哪里、有多大。

ResearchA theoretical study of how self-evolving agents and frozen-policy agents differ in meta-learning and adaptation: an agent with fixed parameters can only adapt through its context, while a self-evolving agent keeps updating itself; where, and by how much, do they differ in learning how to learn?

我的工作想法提出、理论推导、实验设计与运行、论文与报告撰写。

My roleProposed the idea, did the theoretical derivation, designed and ran the experiments, and wrote the paper and reports.

AlpsBench (SIGIR 2026)

2025.09 – 2026.02

科研实习生 · 中国科大数据科学实验室（LDS）Research Intern · Lab for Data Science (LDS), USTC

研究内容基于 WildChat 真实人机对话构建大模型个性化基准：2,500 条长期交互序列配以人工核验的结构化记忆，覆盖个性化信息的抽取、更新、检索与利用四项任务，系统评测前沿大模型与记忆系统。
