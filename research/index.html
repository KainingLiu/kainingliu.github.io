研究Research

我在中国科大数据科学实验室（LDS）从事人工智能记忆与持续学习的研究，导师为王文杰教授。

I work on memory and continual learning for AI at the Lab for Data Science (LDS), USTC, advised by Prof. Wenjie Wang.

研究兴趣Interests

记忆与持续学习Memory & continual learning
让模型原生地积累记忆、在交互中持续学习，而不是一次训练、永久冻结。Models that natively accumulate memory and keep learning through interaction, instead of being trained once and frozen.
自进化与主动学习Self-evolution & active learning
智能体如何主动理解、主动探索，并在使用中改进自身。How agents can actively understand and explore, and improve themselves as they are used.
稀疏计算与类脑智能Sparse computation & brain-inspired intelligence
借鉴人脑稀疏、高效的计算方式构建智能系统。Building intelligent systems that borrow the sparse, efficient computation of the brain.
世界模型World models
对真实世界、具身场景与多感官信息的连贯理解。Coherent understanding of the real world, embodied settings and multi-sensory information.

科研经历Research Experience

LatentTrace

2026.08 – 至今2026.08 – Present

独立负责课题 · 中国科大数据科学实验室（LDS）· 导师：王文杰Independent project · Lab for Data Science (LDS), USTC · Advisor: Prof. Wenjie Wang

  • 目标：让语言模型以参数内的向量记忆替代文本上下文，获得原生的长期记忆与持续学习能力；
  • 方法：在大模型中引入“存储”与“召回”两种动作，逐层把隐状态写入记忆并按需调回；存什么、何时取由模型自己学会，不引入外部检索器或独立的记忆网络；
  • 独立负责问题提出、模型设计、训练方案与评测，工作进行中，尚未公开。
  • Goal: let language models replace textual context with vector memory held in their own parameters, giving them native long-term memory and continual learning.
  • Method: add “store” and “recall” actions to an LLM that write hidden states into memory layer by layer and bring them back on demand; the model learns what to store and when to recall, with no external retriever or separate memory network.
  • I lead the problem formulation, model design, training recipe and evaluation. Work in progress, not yet public.

AlpsBench: An LLM Personalization Benchmark for Real-Dialogue Memorization and Preference Alignment

Jianfei Xiao, Xiang Yu, Chengbing Wang, Wuqiang Zheng, Xinyu Lin, Kaining Liu, Hongxun Ding, Yang Zhang, Wenjie Wang, Fuli Feng, Xiangnan He

SIGIR 2026 · The 49th International ACM SIGIR Conference on Research and Development in Information Retrieval, Melbourne

基于 WildChat 真实对话构建 2,500 条长期对话序列及人工核验的结构化记忆，覆盖记忆抽取、更新、检索与利用四项任务，系统评测大模型与记忆智能体的个性化能力。

A benchmark of 2,500 long-term real-dialogue sequences from WildChat with human-verified structured memories, evaluating LLMs and memory agents on four tasks: memory extraction, updating, retrieval and utilization.
