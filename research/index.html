研究Research

我在中国科大数据科学实验室（LDS）从事人工智能记忆与持续学习的研究，导师为王文杰教授。

I work on memory and continual learning for AI at the Lab for Data Science (LDS), USTC, advised by Prof. Wenjie Wang.

研究兴趣Interests

记忆与持续学习Memory & continual learning
让模型原生地积累记忆、在交互中持续学习，而不是一次训练、永久冻结。Models that natively accumulate memory and keep learning through interaction, instead of being trained once and frozen.
自进化与主动学习Self-evolution & active learning
智能体如何主动理解、主动探索，并在使用中改进自身。How agents can actively understand and explore, and improve themselves as they are used.
稀疏计算与类脑智能Sparse computation & brain-inspired intelligence
借鉴人脑稀疏、高效的计算方式构建智能系统。Building intelligent systems that borrow the sparse, efficient computation of the brain.
世界模型World models
对真实世界、具身场景与多感官信息的连贯理解。Coherent understanding of the real world, embodied settings and multi-sensory information.

科研经历Research Experience

LatentTrace

2026.08 – 至今2026.08 – Present

课题负责人 · 中国科大数据科学实验室（LDS）· 导师：王文杰Project lead · Lab for Data Science (LDS), USTC · Advisor: Prof. Wenjie Wang

研究内容让 AI 模型像人一样拥有自己的记忆。今天的 AI 助手只能参考眼前这段对话，聊得越久越慢越贵，超出长度就得遗忘；本课题让模型把读懂后的“印象”存进一块不断增长的记忆里，通过训练将原生记忆能力融入模型，即使原文已被清空也能凭记忆回答。

ResearchGiving AI models a memory of their own. Today's AI assistants can only look at the current conversation, so long chats get slow and costly and eventually have to be forgotten. This project lets a model store its own “impression” of what it has read in a memory that keeps growing, and training builds this native memory into the model itself, so it can answer from memory even after the original text is gone.

负责部分作为课题负责人，独立完成前期调研、模型设计、训练数据构建和模型训练。

My roleAs project lead, independently carried out the literature survey, model design, training-data construction and model training.

AlpsBench (SIGIR 2026)

2025.09 – 2026.02

科研实习生 · 中国科大数据科学实验室（LDS）Research Intern · Lab for Data Science (LDS), USTC

研究内容一份检验 AI 能不能“记住你、懂你”的考卷：从真实聊天记录中整理出 2,500 段长期对话，考查 AI 能否记下用户信息、及时更新、需要时想起并用好，用来测试当前最先进的 AI 模型和记忆系统。

ResearchA test of whether an AI can remember and understand you: 2,500 long-running conversations from real chat logs, checking whether AI notices user details, keeps them up to date, recalls them when needed and puts them to good use. Used to evaluate today's most advanced AI models and memory systems.
