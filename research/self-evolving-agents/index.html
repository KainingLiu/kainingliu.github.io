自进化智能体的理论研究Theory of Self-Evolving Agents

访问学生 · 北京中关村学院 · 合作老师：陈祥Visiting student · Zhongguancun Academy, Beijing · Host: Xiang Chen

2026 暑期Summer 2026

一句话介绍In one sentence

用数学回答一个问题：会在使用中不断自我改进的 AI，到底比“出厂后就不再变化”的 AI 强在哪里、强多少？

Using mathematics to answer one question: how much better, and in what way, is an AI that keeps improving itself than one that stays fixed after release?

研究背景Background

大多数 AI 智能体部署之后就固定不变，遇到新任务只能靠临时给它的提示和例子去应变；另一类“自进化”智能体则会在使用过程中不断更新自己，越用越好。

Most AI agents are frozen once deployed: facing a new task, they can only adapt using the instructions and examples placed in front of them. “Self-evolving” agents, by contrast, keep updating themselves as they are used and get better over time.

直觉上，后者更会学习、更能适应新环境，但这种优势究竟来自哪里、有多大，一直缺少严格的回答。本研究尝试从理论上把这件事讲清楚，并用实验加以验证。

Intuitively the second kind learns better and adapts better to new situations, but where that advantage comes from, and how large it is, has never been pinned down. This work sets out to answer that rigorously in theory and to check it with experiments.

负责部分My Role

提出研究想法，完成理论推导，设计并运行实验，撰写论文和报告。

Proposed the idea, worked out the theory, designed and ran the experiments, and wrote the paper and reports.
