研究内容Research

从理论上研究自进化智能体与冻结策略智能体在元学习与自适应能力上的差异。

A theoretical study of how self-evolving agents and frozen-policy agents differ in meta-learning and adaptation.

冻结策略的智能体在部署后参数不再改变，面对新任务只能依靠上下文中的信息调整行为；自进化智能体则在使用过程中持续更新自身。本研究关心的问题是：两类智能体在“学会如何学习”和适应新环境的能力上，差别究竟在哪里、有多大。

A frozen-policy agent keeps its parameters fixed after deployment and can only adapt to new tasks through information in its context; a self-evolving agent keeps updating itself as it is used. The question is where, and by how much, the two differ in their ability to learn how to learn and to adapt to new environments.

负责部分My Role

想法提出、理论推导、实验设计与运行、论文与报告撰写。

Proposing the idea, theoretical derivation, designing and running experiments, and writing the paper and reports.
