一句话介绍In one sentence

一份检验 AI 能不能“记住你、懂你”的考卷，用真实聊天记录出题，论文发表于信息检索领域国际顶级会议 SIGIR 2026。

A test of whether an AI can remember and understand you, built from real chat logs and published at SIGIR 2026, a top international conference in information retrieval.

为什么需要它Why it is needed

AI 助手越来越像长期陪伴用户的伙伴，“记住你是谁、懂你喜欢什么”变得越来越重要。但怎样衡量一个 AI 在这方面做得好不好，一直缺少可靠的标准：已有的测试要么不考这件事，要么用的是人工编出来的对话，和真实聊天差别很大。

AI assistants are becoming long-term companions, so remembering who you are and what you like matters more and more. Yet there has been no reliable way to measure how well an AI does this: existing tests either skip it or use made-up conversations that look little like real chats.

我们做了什么What we built

我们从真实的人与 AI 聊天记录中整理出 2,500 段长期对话，并人工核对了每段对话里“AI 应该记住什么”。考卷分四关：

We assembled 2,500 long-running conversations from real human–AI chat logs and checked by hand what an AI should remember from each one. The test has four parts:

  • 记下来：能否从聊天中发现用户的特点和信息；
  • 及时更新：用户情况变了，能否修正旧的记忆；
  • 想得起：能否找出和当前话题相关的记忆；
  • 用得好：能否据此给出真正合用户心意的回答。
  • Notice: can it pick up the user's traits and details from the chat?
  • Update: when the user's situation changes, does it correct what it remembered?
  • Recall: can it find the memories that matter for the current topic?
  • Use: does it turn them into answers that genuinely suit the user?

主要发现What we found

我们用它测试了目前最先进的 AI 模型和记忆系统，发现：用户没有明说的特点，AI 很难察觉；用户情况变化后，即使最强的模型也常常没能更新记忆；记忆一多，AI 就容易找错；有了记忆功能，AI 能想起更多事，但不一定因此更懂用户、更有温度。

Testing today's most advanced AI models and memory systems, we found that AI rarely notices traits users do not state outright; even the strongest models often fail to update when a user's situation changes; with many memories, AI easily picks the wrong one; and while memory features help AI recall more, they do not necessarily make it more understanding or more caring.
