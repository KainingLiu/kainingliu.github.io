我是中国科学技术大学少年班学院 2024 级本科生，主修信息与计算科学。目前在中国科大数据科学实验室（LDS）从事人工智能记忆与持续学习的研究。

我关心的问题是：以人脑为启发，搭建新一代更强大、更可靠、更有趣的智能系统。具体关注的方法有：构建原生的持续学习与记忆能力；主动理解与主动探索的元认知能力与第一性原理的思维能力；对真实世界、具身场景、复杂系统、多感官多维度信息的连贯理解能力。

我的主要精力花在：做人工智能相关的科学研究；与同学们一起做有趣的智能体和机器人项目；探索与思考数学科学问题；参与和组织讲座、讨论、黑客松等活动。

I am an undergraduate (Class of 2024) at the School of the Gifted Young, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), majoring in Information and Computational Science. I currently work on memory and continual learning for AI at the Lab for Data Science (LDS), USTC.

The question I care about is how to build a new generation of intelligent systems, inspired by the human brain, that are more capable, more reliable and more interesting. The directions I focus on: native continual learning and memory; metacognition for active understanding and exploration, together with first-principles thinking; and coherent understanding of the real world, embodied settings, complex systems, and multi-sensory, multi-dimensional information.

Most of my time goes into AI research; building fun agents and robots with fellow students; exploring and thinking about problems in mathematics and science; and attending and organizing talks, discussions and hackathons.
