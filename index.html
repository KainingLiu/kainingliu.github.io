I am an undergraduate (Class of 2024) at the School of the Gifted Young, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), majoring in Information and Computational Science and enrolled in the Interdisciplinary Honors Program in Humanoid Robotics.

Since September 2025 I have been doing research at the Lab for Data Science (LDS), USTC. I contributed to AlpsBench (SIGIR 2026), a benchmark for LLM personalization and memory, and to a personalized memory agent for Huawei XiaoYi. I now lead an independent project on memory-native models. The question I care about: how can intelligent agents keep remembering and keep learning through interaction, as people do?

Outside the lab I build robots and agents with my teammates: Team Uniforest won RoboGame 2026, and our voice-notes device MemoCat won the Grand Prize of the “107 Cup” Computing Power & AI Agent Development Competition.

论文发表Publications

SIGIR 2026

AlpsBench: An LLM Personalization Benchmark for Real-Dialogue Memorization and Preference Alignment

Jianfei Xiao, Xiang Yu, Chengbing Wang, Wuqiang Zheng, Xinyu Lin, Kaining Liu, Hongxun Ding, Yang Zhang, Wenjie Wang, Fuli Feng, Xiangnan He

Proceedings of the 49th International ACM SIGIR Conference on Research and Development in Information Retrieval (SIGIR 2026), Melbourne, Australia

基于 WildChat 真实对话构建了 2,500 条长期对话序列及人工核验的结构化记忆，覆盖记忆抽取、更新、检索与利用四项任务，系统评测大模型与记忆智能体的个性化能力。

A benchmark of 2,500 long-term real-dialogue sequences from WildChat with human-verified structured memories, evaluating LLMs and memory agents on four tasks: memory extraction, updating, retrieval and utilization.
